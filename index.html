About

Results-driven Engineering in Structural Steel Fabrication

I'm a results-driven Production Engineer with a proven track record of optimizing production processes for a structural steel fabricator in Melbourne. I prepare quotations and project schedules from engineering drawings, manage material and inventory flow, and apply lean manufacturing principles to shorten timeframes, cut waste, and maintain cGMP, quality, and regulatory compliance across the warehouse floor.

I hold a Master of Engineering Science from Swinburne University of Technology and a Bachelor of Engineering Science from the University of Engineering & Technology, Lahore, and I'm recognised as a Professional Engineer by Engineers Australia. Beyond the technical work, I bring strong collaboration and communication skills to train and mentor shop-floor staff, liaise with clients and suppliers, and support plant and facilities maintenance using AutoCAD and SolidWorks.
